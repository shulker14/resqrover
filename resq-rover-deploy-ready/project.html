RESQ / STUDENT ROBOTICS PROJECT

Meet Rover.

ResQ Rover is a student-built autonomous rescue-rover concept focused on perception, conversation, and the handoff of useful information. The project explores what a small robot can learn before a person has to enter an uncertain environment.

01

Explore

Move through areas that may be difficult or risky to inspect first.

02

Detect

Use onboard vision to identify people and estimate useful context.

03

Interact

Use a constrained AI conversation to gather information and keep the person engaged.
